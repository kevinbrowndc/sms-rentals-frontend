About RentalSMS

RentalSMS is an independent SMS notification service designed to help renters find available housing faster.

Our Mission

Rental markets move quickly. By the time many listings are discovered, they are already under application. RentalSMS was created to provide timely, opt-in SMS alerts when new rental listings match a user’s selected location and budget.

How RentalSMS Works

Users voluntarily sign up on our website and provide their mobile phone number, city, and budget preferences. When new publicly available rental listings match those preferences, RentalSMS sends an informational SMS alert.

Message frequency may vary and may be up to three messages per day depending on listing activity.
