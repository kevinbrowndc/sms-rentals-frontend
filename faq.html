Frequently Asked Questions

What is RentalSMS?

RentalSMS is an informational SMS notification service that alerts subscribers when new rental listings match their selected city and budget preferences.

How does RentalSMS work?

Users voluntarily submit their phone number and rental preferences through our website and check a consent box. When new publicly available rental listings match those preferences, RentalSMS sends an informational SMS alert.

How often will I receive messages?

Message frequency varies depending on listing activity. You may receive up to three messages per day.

Is RentalSMS a real estate company?

No. RentalSMS is not a real estate broker, property manager, or listing platform. We do not own or manage rental properties. We provide informational alerts only.

How do I unsubscribe?

You may opt out at any time by replying STOP to any RentalSMS message. You will no longer receive messages unless you opt in again.

How do I get help?

Reply HELP to any message or email support@rentalsms.com.

Does RentalSMS sell my information?

No. RentalSMS does not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing or advertising purposes.

Are there fees?

RentalSMS may offer free or paid subscription plans. Any pricing will be clearly disclosed before billing occurs. Standard carrier message and data rates may apply.
