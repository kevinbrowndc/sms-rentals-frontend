RentalSMS

RentalSMS provides opt-in SMS alerts when new rental listings matching your selected city and budget become available. This is an informational rental notification service.

SMS Consent & Messaging Disclosure

How RentalSMS Works

1. You enter your city and budget.
2. RentalSMS monitors publicly available listings.
3. When a match is detected, you receive an informational SMS alert.
4. You may unsubscribe anytime by replying STOP.

Example SMS

Rental Alert: New 2BR apartment in Austin listed at $1,650. Reply STOP to unsubscribe. Reply HELP for help.
